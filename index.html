How RFM Metrics & Feature Scaling Are Calculated ▼

To cluster customers accurately, raw transaction rows are aggregated per unique customer ID and transformed using the following mathematical pipeline:

1. Recency (R)

Days since last purchase relative to max date in file.

R = (MaxDate + 1) - LastOrderDate
2. Frequency (F)

Total count of distinct invoice numbers per customer.

F = CountDistinct(OrderID)
3. Monetary (M)

Cumulative revenue generated across all transactions.

M = Sum(UnitPrice × Quantity)
Why Log Transformation & Z-Score Scaling Are Essential

A. Log Transformation: E-commerce spending has extreme high-end outliers. Applying log(1 + x) compresses long-tailed distributions into a normal curve.

B. Z-Score Standardization: Since Monetary spend ranges in thousands ($) while Frequency ranges from 1 to 10, spend would artificially dominate K-Means. We scale all metrics to Mean = 0 and StdDev = 1 using: Z = (X - Mean) / StdDev.
